Adam Zhu

Adam Zhu is an undergraduate at Carnegie Mellon University studying Statistics and Machine Learning. His work includes usage-analytics research at NIST, TrueLine (an iOS computer-vision bowling tracker), forensic image research at CSAFE, statistical analysis of VR evacuation studies, and a co-authored publication on matched binary diagnostic data.

Selected work

  • NIST, Research Data and Computing Office (research intern, May to August 2026): built a five-stage Python pipeline (pandas, scikit-learn) that separates automated from human traffic across 1.04M requests to NIST's public data portal (271 days, 117K unique IPs). A random forest using only behavioral signals reached F1 = 0.94. Caught a disguised scraper that hit the portal monthly from fresh IPs and spoofed hundreds of browser user-agents; an isolation forest surfaced it and log investigation confirmed it. Turning that into auditable cleaning rules raised the measured automated share of traffic from 21.7% to 41.9%. Found that 21.5% of 109K human searches returned no results and built a TF-IDF query-failure classifier (F1 0.80).
  • TrueLine (Jan 2026–present): iOS app that measures a bowling ball's launch speed, breakpoint, and entry angle with one iPhone instead of a $10k+ in-lane system. Tracked the ball down the full lane on 6 of 6 unseen clips (two alleys, three camera angles) with a YOLOv8n detector fine-tuned on 215 labeled frames, exported to a 5.9 MB Core ML model with Kalman-filter tracking, fully on-device. A four-corner homography calibrates any camera angle into lane coordinates; a clip-by-clip harness verifies the Swift port against the Python prototype within 1 board and 1–2% on speed. Swift, SwiftUI, Core ML, AVFoundation, PyTorch, OpenCV. GitHub repo. Released on the App Store in August 2026.
  • Clarity (Sep 2026): a macOS menu-bar app built by four people at HackCMU in two days. Press ⌘⇧E on any problem on screen, drag a box around it, and get a written explanation in seconds and an animation generated for that exact problem (a model writes Manim code, a sandbox renders it) about a minute later. Adam built the desktop app: menu bar, global hotkey, region capture, the spotlight and result windows, local recents, and the signed app bundle and DMG. Python, pywebview, Go, LangGraph, Docker. Clarity's site, GitHub repo.
  • ScottyLabs AI, Carnegie Mellon (software engineer, Sep 2026–present): set up the dev stack for Bark, CMU's campus AI assistant (Python LLM backend, MCP tools, Postgres).
  • CSAFE, Iowa State: forensic analysis on 24,000+ camera images for device fingerprinting and handwriting similarity datasets; co-author on a manuscript under review at Forensic Sciences.
  • VR Applications Center (VRAC), Iowa State: regression models of evacuation time across 454 trials and 227 participants, testing automated communication strategies in simulated school-shooting evacuations; co-author on a manuscript in preparation.
  • COR Robotics: co-designed curriculum and taught hands-on robotics (drones, engineering) to 100+ students in grades 3–8.
  • Team Neutrino #3928 (FIRST): co-captain coordinating seven sub-teams and 40+ members; multiple NASA Engineering Inspiration Awards and Worlds qualifications.
  • Publication: Lin, H., Zhu, A., Wang, C. (2024). "Statistical Tests for Proportion Difference in One-to-Two Matched Binary Diagnostic Data: Application to Environmental Testing of Salmonella in the United States." Mathematics 12(5), 741. doi.org/10.3390/math12050741.
